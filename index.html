A ranked list of the Fast and Furious Movies

The Fast and the Furious series is the greatest movie franchise to exist Not only are there 10 movies with exciting action and adventures; but there is also great music and visuals. All of this is topped off with emotional messages and connections to found family.

These are my favorite movies, so I have strong opinions on which are best. At the end of the day though they are all amazing.
